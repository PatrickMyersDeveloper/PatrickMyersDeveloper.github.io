Allow Me to Introduce Myself

Patrick Myers

My story starts in an obscure corner of the U.S. most aren't very familiar with: the Upper Peninsula of Michigan (often referred to as the U.P.). For those unfamiliar with it, this section of Michigan makes up about 40% of the state's land, but only 3% of the population. Given that fact, you'd be correct to assume this area is largely rural and untouched. The rare travel excursions of my childhood consisted mostly of driving around nearby Midwestern states like Wisconsin and Minnesota, and going to visit my extended family in metro Detroit. Growing up in that environment left me sheltered and itching to experience the spectrum of places I had only seen on TV or heard about in passing.

From a very young age, I had a fascination with the people and places that existed beyond the world I knew. I credit this to a small handful of people, namely my parents and a person my dad also greatly admires: Anthony Bourdain. Let me start with Mom and Dad. My parents were a bit more worldly than most of the local Yoopers (natives of the U.P) that were abundant in my neighborhood. Between the two of them, they had spent a number of years living in places like Portland, Detroit, Los Angeles, and Boston. Because of this, they had broader perspectives and experiences surrounding people, culture, and food than most others in my impossibly small town. Bourdain on the other hand was introduced to me by my father through weeknight cable reruns of No Reservations and eventually Parts Unknown. As I got a bit older, I began reading portions of his writing as well. I was always in such awe of the destinations Bourdain found himself in, largely because I had never had any other frame of reference for these places. He made a career of showcasing corners of the world often forgotten or unknown by the vast majority. I was deeply fascinated by places like Vietnam, Bhutan, Democratic Republic of Congo, and Lebanon. The way Bourdain showcased culture as a combination of history, food, geography, and politics was critical for how I began to see the world. For all of those years spent growing up in Michigan, I yearned for a future where I'd be able to absorb all of these offbeat destinations firsthand.

After high school, I started travelling as much as I could. I went abroad for the first time in 2019, travelling to Finland, Denmark, Estonia, and Sweden. Eventually, I was fortunate enough to meet some of my best friends and travel partners during my college years in Marquette, MI. Driving around the U.S. in my Toyota RAV4 became a staple of our lives.

Today, I spend most of my spare time and money furthering my goal of exploring. Gaining the opportunity to see so many interesting places, talk to strangers, share meals, and seek a deeper understanding of who they are has been the greatest pleasure of my young life. It's with the purpose of remembering those people, the meals we shared, and the perspective they gave me that I have created this project. With that, I present Leave the World Behind.
